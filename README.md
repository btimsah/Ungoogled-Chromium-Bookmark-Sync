## Getting Started

This extension requires a **free Aptosity account** to act as the sync engine.

### 1. Download the Extension

**Download it here:**

### [⬇️ Download Helium / Ungoogled Chromium Sync](https://github.com/btimsah/Ungoogled-Chromium-Bookmark-Sync/raw/refs/heads/main/helium-browser-sync.zip)

Save the `.zip` file somewhere on your computer.

### 2. Extract the ZIP

Before installing the extension, **extract the ZIP file**.

Right-click the downloaded `helium-browser-sync.zip` file and choose **Extract All**.

Keep the extracted folder somewhere permanent. Do not delete or move it after installing the extension.

### 3. Load the Extension in Developer Mode

Because Ungoogled Chromium and Helium may not have access to the Chrome Web Store, the extension is installed manually:

1. Open your browser.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/`.
3. Enable **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked**.
5. Select the **extracted folder** — do not select the `.zip` file itself.
6. The Aptosity extension should now appear in your installed extensions.
7. Pin the Aptosity extension to your toolbar.

> **Important:** The ZIP must be extracted first. Use **Load unpacked** on the extracted folder, not on `helium-browser-sync.zip`.

### 4. Connect Your Browser

Once the extension is installed:

1. Sign up or log in at [**Aptosity.com/sync**](https://aptosity.com/sync).
2. Open the **Config** tab on the web dashboard.
3. Copy your unique **Sync Token**.
4. Click the Aptosity extension icon in your browser toolbar.
5. Open **Config/Settings**.
6. Paste your Sync Token.
7. Click **Import from Browser** to push your current local bookmarks to the cloud.

> **No local bookmarks?**  
> Go to the web dashboard's **Config** tab and upload your exported `bookmarks.html` file.

> **Want to back up your extensions?**  
> In the browser extension popup, click **Sync Extensions**. This saves your installed extensions as URLs in an **Extensions** folder.

## Managing Your Data

You are always in control of your synchronization data.

- **Export** — Download a standard `bookmarks.html` file at any time and import it into any browser's native bookmark manager.
- **Wipe Profile** — Instantly clear your Aptosity profile from the server.  
  *Wiping does not affect bookmarks already stored in your browser; it only clears the cloud copy.*
- **Revoke Token** — Regenerate your Sync Token to instantly sever the connection between your browser and the server.

`https://aptosity.com/sync/`

Use a custom new-tab extension to set your home page to:

`https://aptosity.com/sync/`

### Exporting Bookmarks

In standard Chrome, Brave, or Edge:

1. Open the Bookmark Manager with `Ctrl+Shift+O`.
2. Click the `⋮` menu.
3. Select **Export bookmarks**.
4. Save the resulting `bookmarks.html` file.
